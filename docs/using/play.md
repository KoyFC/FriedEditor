# Play

Play (F5, or the green button in the toolbar) saves the scene, builds the project's game for the PC in its `build/` folder, which needs the same tools as the Project Manager's build, and runs it. The button becomes Stop while the game builds or runs, and the game's log is `fried-editor-game.log` in the temporary directory. Ctrl+S saves the scene to `assets/scenes/` in the project.

A build, of the player or of the game, opens a window with its step, a bar and the end of its log, and a failed one stays open with the error until it is closed.
